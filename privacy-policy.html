SIMPRO_CALCULATOR

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short

SIMPRO_CALCULATOR does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no trackers. Everything you type stays on your own device.

What the calculator keeps on your device

  • Your settings: angle unit, display format, decimal places, how results are shown and text size. They are kept in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time. They are never sent anywhere.
  • A saved copy of the calculator: the program files are saved on your device so the calculator opens with no internet.
  • Your calculations (the history, tables, matrices, spreadsheet cells and so on) exist only while the calculator is open. They are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers or anything you calculate. The calculator does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Where the calculator is hosted

The calculator is delivered as a website hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host may record technical information such as your IP address when the pages are loaded. We do not receive or use that information. See GitHub's own privacy statement on github.com for how GitHub handles it.

Google Play and your browser

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own policies. The app shows the calculator website using your device's browser, which is covered by that browser's own privacy policy.

Children

SIMPRO_CALCULATOR is a general learning tool. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

  • To remove your saved settings and the saved copy of the calculator, clear the site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Inside the calculator, SHIFT then MENU (SET UP) then Reset all settings puts the settings back to normal.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
